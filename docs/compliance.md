# Record checks and their sources

`npm run stays -- compliance` (the `/compliance` command) checks the records against the rules below. Each check is a query in the `v_compliance` view in `supabase/migrations/0001_short_stay.sql`; the night counts come from `v_night_cap`. A clean result means the records are complete and consistent with the rule as written here. It does not mean the home is lawful: planning approvals, fire safety and strata by-laws are your call, and your duty.

Each home carries a `rules` value that says which of these apply to it: `nsw-cap`, `nsw`, `byron-cap`, `wa-perth`, `wa-regional`, `vic`, `qldc`, `other` or `none`. Set it when you add the home.

Sources were read on 12 November 2026. Rules change, and several of these are under review. If a source says something different from this page, the source wins: update this page and the check together (`/customise`).

| Rule | What the data must show | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `NSW-180-NIGHTS` | A non-hosted home in Greater Sydney (and the other mapped areas) is let for no more than 180 nights in its counting year. Bookings of 21 consecutive nights or more are left out of the count. The check counts nights let and nights already booked ahead, so you see the breach before it happens. Hosted homes (`--hosted`) are not counted. Owner blocks are not lettings and are not counted. Set the counting year with `cap_year_starts_on` (your registration date); without it the check counts from 1 January. | [NSW Planning: Short-term rental accommodation](https://planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/housing/short-term-rental-accommodation) |
| `BYRON-60-NIGHTS` | A non-hosted home in most of Byron Shire is let for no more than 60 nights a year. The two 365-day precincts (Byron Bay town centre and Brunswick Heads) are not capped: give those homes rules `nsw` instead. The cap applies from the first renewal on or after 23 September 2024. | [NSW Planning: Byron Shire](https://planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/housing/short-term-rental-accommodation/byron-shire) |
| `STRA-REGISTRATION` | Every NSW and WA home has a registration number recorded, and it has not expired. The check warns 30 days before expiry. In WA the number must be shown, readily visible, on every advertisement for the home; in NSW the register number goes on every listing. | [WA STRA Register FAQ](https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/department-of-local-government-industry-regulation-and-safety/short-term-rental-accommodation-register-frequently-asked-questions), [NSW Planning: Short-term rental accommodation](https://planning.nsw.gov.au/the-planning-system/housing/short-term-rental-accommodation) |
| `WA-PERTH-90-NIGHTS` | An unhosted home in the Perth metropolitan area needs development approval once it is let for more than 90 nights (not consecutive) in a 12-month period counted from its registration date. Record the approval with `set home <name> --approval=<reference>` and the check stops counting. Outside Perth, your local government decides: give those homes rules `wa-regional` and add a cap with `--night-cap` if your council sets one. | [WA Planning: STRA planning reforms](https://www.planning.wa.gov.au/planning-reform/short-term-rental-accommodation-planning-reforms), [WA STRA Register FAQ](https://www.wa.gov.au/organisation/department-of-local-government-industry-regulation-and-safety/short-term-rental-accommodation-register-frequently-asked-questions) |
| `VIC-SHORT-STAY-LEVY` | Every stay of less than 28 nights in a Victorian home, completed on or after 1 January 2025, that was booked directly (not through a platform that collects the levy itself), has the levy recorded. The levy is 7.5% of the total booking fee: the nightly rate, cleaning fees, GST and any late checkout fee. A platform booking is the platform's to collect: mark the channel `collects_levy`. `npm run stays -- levy` totals it by quarter. | [State Revenue Office Victoria: Understanding short stay levy](https://www.sro.vic.gov.au/owning-property/short-stay-levy/understanding-short-stay-levy) |
| `QLDC-REGISTERED` and `QLDC-NIGHTS` | A home let as visitor accommodation in a Queenstown Lakes residential zone is registered with the council before letting starts, and stays within the night limit for its zone. The limit depends on the zone (90 nights in most residential zones after the February 2023 Environment Court decision, fewer in some), so set `--night-cap` per home from the District Plan for its zone. The council also asks for letting records each year: `npm run docs -- letting-record` prints them. | [QLDC: Changes to visitor accommodation rules follow Environment Court decision (1 February 2023)](https://www.qldc.govt.nz/2023/february/23-02-01-changes-to-visitor-accommodation-rules-follow-environment-court-decision) |
| `DOUBLE-BOOKING` | No home is sold twice for the same night, and no reservation sits over an owner block. | Operator policy |
| `CARD-DATA` | No guest or reservation note holds something shaped like a card number. Card numbers belong in your payment provider, not in free text. | Operator policy, with [Privacy Principle 5 (storage and security)](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) and [APP 11](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-quick-reference) |
| `PRIVACY-RETENTION` | No guest whose last stay ended more than `privacy_review_years` ago (default 7) is kept without a decision. Keep the money records your accountant needs; anonymise the guest. | [Privacy Commissioner: Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/), [OAIC: APP 11.2](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-quick-reference) |

## What the checks do not do

- They do not register a home, lodge a levy return or report bookings to a government register. Platforms that are integrated with the NSW register report their own bookings; for direct bookings you enter them yourself.
- They do not check fire safety standards, strata by-laws, council consents outside the rules above, GST on your fees, or trust account rules for holding guest money. Add a rule with `/customise` when your business needs one, with its source.
- They read your records. A booking taken in a channel and never imported is invisible to them.

Nothing here is legal or tax advice.
